Workflow 1

Auto Resume Optimizer

Send a LinkedIn job URL to your Telegram bot. The pipeline extracts the JD, researches the company, tailors your resume, saves it to Google Docs, and sends the link back — all automatically.

1
Trigger · Telegram
Receive LinkedIn URL
Your Telegram bot listens for incoming messages. When a LinkedIn job URL is detected, it's added to a processing queue.
→ Telegram component
2
Extractor · LinkedIn
Extract Job Description
Scrapes the LinkedIn job page and uses an LLM to produce a clean, structured job description with title, company, responsibilities, and requirements.
→ LinkedIn component
3
Research · Web Search
Research Company Fit
Searches DuckDuckGo, TeamBlind, and Levels.fyi to gather culture signals, compensation data, and overall company fit scored against your preferences.
→ Web Search component
4
LLM · Agent
Optimize Resume
Sends the JD, company analysis, and your base LaTeX resume to Claude. The model rewrites the summary and reorders bullets to align with this specific role.
→ Agent component
5
Output · Google Docs
Save to Google Docs
Converts the optimized LaTeX to a Google Doc named Company - Role Title and generates a shareable link.
→ Google Docs component
✓
Deliver · Telegram
Send Results Back
Sends the Google Doc link and company fit summary back to your Telegram chat. Job done.
→ Telegram component

Setup Required

Point your Telegram bot webhook to /webhook/telegram on this server. Google Docs auth must be connected. URLs are queued and processed one at a time.
